Privacy policy

Sprint Recap · Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Publisher: Cloverkite

Sprint Recap ("the app") is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira Cloud. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It runs entirely on Atlassian's platform ("Runs on Atlassian") and has no servers of its own.

What the app reads

When you open the Sprint Recap page, your browser asks Jira, as you, for:

  • the project's scrum board and its sprints (names, goals, dates);
  • the issues in the selected sprint (key, summary, status, type, creation date, sprint and flagged fields);
  • the history of those issues' status, sprint and flagged fields.

You only see what your own Jira permissions already allow. The data is used to build the page on your screen and is discarded when you close or reload it.

What the app does not do

  • No storage: nothing is saved by the app (no database, no Forge storage, no cookies, no local storage).
  • No transfer: no data is sent outside your Atlassian site. The app makes no calls to external services.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no advertising, no logging of user data.
  • No changes: the app is read-only and never modifies your Jira data.

Permissions

The app requests read-only access: read:jira-work, read:board-scope:jira-software, read:sprint:jira-software, read:project:jira.

Data held by Atlassian

Your Jira data stays under Atlassian's terms and privacy policy. Atlassian may record that the app is installed and how it's used, as for any Marketplace app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: cloverkite.hello@proton.me.

Changes

If the app ever starts storing or sending data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be listed in the release notes.
