Sprint Recap for Jira

What got done, what slipped and why, in plain words.

After every sprint, someone has to explain to the rest of the company what happened. Sprint Recap does it on one page, for people who don't live in Jira.

  • The headline: "7 of 10 items finished · 3 moving to next sprint", with the sprint goal and dates.
  • Not finished, and why: added mid-sprint, blocked, not started, or where it stood at the end. Repeat slips show "2nd sprint".
  • Progress you can't see: unfinished items that still moved forward.
  • Summary for Slack or email, and a dashboard gadget.

Read-only. Runs on Atlassian: no outside servers, nothing stored. No setup.
