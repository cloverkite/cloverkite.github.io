Getting started

Sprint Recap works on the sprints you already have, past ones included. There is nothing to set up.

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace. A Jira admin approves it once.
  2. Open a Jira project that has a scrum board.
  3. In the project's tabs, open Sprint Recap (it may be under "More"). It shows the last closed sprint first; pick any other sprint in the picker.
  4. To share it with people outside Jira, click Summary for Slack or email and paste the text where they read.
  5. For a dashboard, add the Sprint Recap gadget and choose a project.

People who can see a project in Jira can see its recap. Sprint Recap is read-only: it never changes your Jira data.

FAQ

Why don't I see Sprint Recap in my project?
It's a project page: look in the project tabs, sometimes under "More". The project needs a scrum board (Kanban boards have no sprints).
What counts as "finished"?
Items whose status was in a "Done" category when the sprint closed. For the active sprint, the status right now.
What do the reasons mean?
  • Added mid-sprint: created in, or moved into, the sprint more than an hour after it started.
  • Blocked: flagged in Jira, or in a status whose name contains "block", when the sprint closed.
  • Not started: still in a "To Do" status, with no status change during the sprint.
  • Status at end: X: the item's status when the sprint closed ("Status now" for the active sprint).
What is "2nd sprint" / "3rd sprint"?
How many sprints the item has been part of, counting this one.
What is "Progress you can't see"?
Unfinished items that still moved forward during the sprint, e.g. from To Do to In Progress.
Why aren't sub-tasks counted?
Like Jira's own sprint report, the recap counts stories, tasks and bugs, not their sub-tasks.
Why does "Summary for Slack or email" show text instead of copying it?
Jira doesn't let this kind of app page write to your clipboard. Select the text and press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on a Mac).
My project has several scrum boards.
The recap currently uses the project's first scrum board.
"You don't have permission" message.
The app sees only what you can see in Jira. Ask a Jira admin for access to the board and its project.
Do I have to wait for a new sprint to try it?
No. Past sprints work right away: pick any closed sprint in the picker. The recap is rebuilt from each item's Jira history, so it shows the status as it was when that sprint closed.
Can people who don't use Jira see the recap?
The page and the gadget are visible to people with access to the project in Jira. For everyone else, use "Summary for Slack or email" and paste the text where they already read.
How does the free trial work?
Atlassian runs the trial and billing. Start it from the Marketplace listing; when it ends, a Jira admin subscribes in Atlassian Administration > Apps. Sites with up to 10 users can use Sprint Recap for free. Prices are on the listing's Pricing tab. Like all Marketplace cloud apps, the price is based on the number of users on the Jira site.
What happens when the trial ends?
The page shows "Sprint Recap needs an active subscription" until a Jira admin subscribes. The app stores nothing, so there's no data to lose or export.
Does the app store or send my data?
No. See the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Please include the sprint name, what you expected, what the page showed, and the "Details" line from any error message. Don't include personal data you don't need to share.
